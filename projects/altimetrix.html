FORMAT

Analytics platform concept

DESIGN FOCUS

Information hierarchy and data visualization

01 / THE CONTEXT

Make the important signals easier to find.

Analytics views can become dense quickly. Altimetrix explores how a team can scan a high-level picture first, then move into the supporting detail without losing context.

The interface pairs a broad desktop overview with a compact mobile companion view. The figures shown here are placeholders, not measured product data.

02 / DESIGN FOCUS

A consistent visual hierarchy.

Primary indicators sit above the trend view, while date controls remain close to the information they affect. A restrained palette separates the interface chrome from the data layer.

01

Scan first

Lead with summary indicators before asking people to read the full chart.

02

Keep context

Keep the selected time period visible near the overview it shapes.

03

Adapt the view

Keep the mobile summary focused instead of shrinking the desktop layout.

03 / NEXT QUESTIONS

Validate the reading order with real tasks.

A useful next step would be testing whether people can identify a change, compare periods, and locate its supporting detail. Real data and user research would shape the final hierarchy.
