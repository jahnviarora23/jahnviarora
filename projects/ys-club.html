YS Club

FORMAT

Movement community concept

DESIGN FOCUS

Progress, participation, and belonging

01 / THE CONTEXT

Make progress feel personal and shared.

YS Club explores a digital home for movement, where a member can understand their own progress and feel connected to a wider community. The experience balances personal feedback with a welcoming club identity.

The mobile concept gives the weekly activity summary a clear place, with a direct path to more detailed activity.

02 / DESIGN FOCUS

Encouragement without clutter.

A distinct badge, soft color fields, and a simple progress indicator make the concept feel energetic without competing with the member’s activity information.

01

03 / NEXT QUESTIONS

Learn what motivates different members.

Further research could explore how members define progress, what kinds of community participation feel supportive, and how to make activity information accessible to different abilities.
