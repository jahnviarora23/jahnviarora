arivaX Dashboard

FORMAT

Travel discovery dashboard concept

DESIGN FOCUS

Inspiration and trip-planning clarity

01 / THE CONTEXT

Turn a spark of inspiration into a next step.

Travel planning moves between open-ended discovery and practical decisions. This dashboard concept starts with a strong sense of place, then gives the visitor a clear invitation to explore further.

The visual direction uses landscape, restrained copy, and a single prominent action to keep the initial view focused.

02 / DESIGN FOCUS

Keep discovery inviting and useful.

Editorial imagery sets the mood, while concise navigation keeps the experience grounded. The main action sits over the landscape as a readable, high-contrast panel.

01

Lead with place

Use destination imagery to make the experience immediately recognizable.

02

Reduce the first step

Make the next exploration action easy to spot and understand.

03

Leave room to browse

Keep the first view calm so discovery can continue naturally.

03 / NEXT QUESTIONS

Connect inspiration to real planning needs.

Further design work could test how people move from a destination idea to practical details such as timing, route, and saved places. Those planning flows are not represented as completed features in this concept.
